Filed Away

HSA receipts, kept as plain files that outlive the app.

Pay now, reimburse decades later

The IRS sets no deadline for reimbursing yourself from an HSA. Keep the records, let the money grow, reimburse whenever you want. Filed Away keeps the records.

Your files, your folder

Receipts are saved as ordinary images in a folder you can open in the Files app, alongside a plain index.csv you can read in any spreadsheet. There is no account and no server — we never see any of it.

Built to outlive the app

One purchase, no subscription. If this app disappears in twenty years, your folder still opens and still makes sense.

What the folder looks like

Everything the app writes is readable without it:

  • index.csv — one row per receipt, opens in any spreadsheet
  • 2026/ — the receipt images, named YYYY-MM-DD_Provider_$Amount.jpg
  • EOB/ — insurance Explanation of Benefits documents
  • Trash/ — deleted items, kept rather than erased

Not tax advice

Filed Away stores records. It does not give tax, legal, medical, or financial advice, and it does not decide whether an expense qualifies for reimbursement.
