WHAT THIS APP IS

Cathode is a display toy. It fills the screen of your iPhone or iPad with animated visual effects — falling glyphs, starfields, particle systems, plasma fields and so on — that are drawn live on the device while you watch. There is nothing else to it. It stores no information about you, connects to no server, and has no account.

Everything Cathode shows is generated by the app itself. Nothing on screen is real data, a real system, or a real connection, and none of it should be presented to anyone as though it were. The console text, network traces, radar contacts and readouts are decorative fiction.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Cathode is not a diagnostic, monitoring, measurement, security, or communications tool, and it does not do anything to your device beyond drawing on the screen. Do not use it, or any screenshot or recording of it, to suggest that a system has been accessed, monitored, compromised, or repaired.

PHOTOSENSITIVITY

Some effects flicker, strobe, or change brightness rapidly. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing imagery, use the Speed and Glow controls to slow and soften the effects, or do not use the app. Stop immediately if you feel unwell.

Effects also keep the display awake and can raise it to full brightness. Running one for a long time will use noticeably more battery and can make the device warm. Both behaviours are switches you control in Settings.
