Cathode

Twenty full-screen effects, drawn live on your device. Offline, no account.

What it is

Cathode fills your iPhone or iPad with an animated effect and gets out of the way. Falling glyphs, a code editor typing itself out, a wall of monitors working a system open, an oscilloscope, a strange attractor, a fractal zoom — twenty in all, each drawn live at full frame rate. Nothing here is looping video and nothing is a static wallpaper.

How it works

Pick an effect and it goes full screen: no controls on top of it, the display kept awake, and — if you want — the brightness turned up. Tap once and a small control bar fades in; leave it alone and it fades back out. Every effect has speed, density, glow, colour and style controls, and you can save what you like as a named preset.

What it will not do

  • It does not go online. No account, no server, no network requests of any kind, and nothing is collected — not even anonymous usage statistics.
  • It does not ask for a single permission. No camera, no microphone, no photos, no location. It never needs one.
  • It is not a tool. The console text, network traces, radar contacts and progress bars are decorative fiction. Nothing on screen is a real system or a real connection, and none of it should be presented to anyone as though it were.

Photosensitivity

Some effects flicker, strobe, or change brightness quickly. If you are sensitive to flashing imagery, use the Speed and Glow controls to slow and soften them, or do not use the app. Effects also keep the display awake and can make the device warm; both are switches you control.

Make the rain say something

Digital Rain can fall in ten scripts — Katakana, Japanese, Chinese, Hangul, Vietnamese, Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, hex or binary. Let it fall as random characters, as good wishes written in that language, or as anything you type yourself: whatever you enter falls in order, so it reads top to bottom.

Free and paid

Digital Rain is free forever, at full quality, with every setting unlocked — no watermark, no time limit, no advertising. Cathode Unlimited is a single one-time payment that unlocks the other nineteen effects and raises the saved-preset limit. It is not a subscription, it does not renew, and it is shareable through Family Sharing.
