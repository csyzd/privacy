Not For Me

Check any label against your own food rules. Offline, no account.

Your rules, not ours

Save the words that matter to you — what you avoid, what you are cutting back on, what you look for. Every label gets checked against your words, not somebody else's idea of healthy.

Everything stays on this device

The food database ships inside the app. There is no account, no server, and no internet connection at any point. Your rules never leave this phone.

Add your first preference

Type one thing you avoid. The app expands it into related ingredient names so you catch it under all its aliases, and you can switch off any that catch the wrong thing.

How it works

You save the words that matter to you — gluten, palm oil, added sugar — and Sift expands each one into the ingredient names it hides behind. Search a product and Sift marks those words right inside the ingredient list.

What it will not do

  • It is not an allergy tool. Ingredient records come from a public dataset, are missing for a few products, and many have not been updated in years. Manufacturers change recipes without telling anyone.
  • It does not judge your diet. There is no score, no green-is-good, no advice. It reports word matches against rules you wrote.
  • It does not go online. The food database ships inside the app. There is no account and no server.

Where the data comes from

USDA FoodData Central, a public domain dataset published by the United States Department of Agriculture. The publication date of the copy in your app is shown in Settings. Always check the physical packaging — it is the only current source of truth.
