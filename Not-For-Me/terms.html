WHAT THIS APP IS

Not For Me is a reference tool. You save words describing foods you avoid, are cutting back on, or are looking for. Not For Me searches a copy of the USDA FoodData Central database that ships inside the app and shows you where those words appear in a product's ingredient list.

Not For Me does not give medical, nutritional, dietary, or health advice, and it does not tell you whether any food is safe or good for you. It reports word matches. Deciding what to eat is yours alone.

NOT AN ALLERGY OR MEDICAL TOOL

Do not use Not For Me to manage a food allergy, intolerance, or any medical condition. Ingredient records in the underlying dataset are supplied by manufacturers, are missing for some products, and many have not been updated in several years. Manufacturers change recipes without notifying the dataset.

A result of 'Fits' means only that none of your words appeared in the stored ingredient text. It is not a statement that a product is safe for you. Always read the physical packaging: it is the only current source of truth for what is in a product. If you have a food allergy or a medical dietary requirement, follow the guidance of a qualified professional.

FOOD DATA

Food data comes from USDA FoodData Central, a public domain dataset published by the United States Department of Agriculture. The USDA does not endorse Not For Me and is not involved in it. The data is provided as published, without verification by us. The publication date of the copy inside your app is shown in Settings.
