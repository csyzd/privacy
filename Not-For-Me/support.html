Where is my data?

In the vault folder you chose when you first opened the app — on your device or in your iCloud Drive. Open the Files app and look for the Not For Me folder. Deleting the app does not delete that folder.

I got a new phone

If your vault is in iCloud Drive, install the app and point it at the same folder. Tap Restore purchase in Settings to bring Pro across.

The app says my ledger can't be read

Your receipt files are safe — only index.csv failed to parse, usually because it was edited outside the app. Fix or remove that file and reopen; the app can rebuild rows from the receipt files themselves.
