Kindergarten math, disguised as a space trip

Your child flies from the launch pad to Jupiter, surveying one site at a time. Every question is read aloud and answered by tapping or dragging, so a child who cannot read yet can play the whole thing alone. The math follows the Common Core kindergarten standards: counting, comparing, adding and subtracting within ten, making ten, teen numbers, measurement, sorting, and shapes.

No account, no ads, no internet

The app contains no networking code at all. Nothing about your child is collected, transmitted, or stored anywhere but on your own iPad. There are no advertisements, no analytics, no third-party code, and no in-app purchases — the app is bought once and that is the end of it.

What you get as a parent

A Parent Zone behind a reading-based gate your child cannot pass shows which skills they have mastered, how many problems they have answered, and how many different days they have played. You can adjust narration, sound, and a daily time limit there.

What your child practises

All seventeen skills follow the Common Core kindergarten standards: counting objects and counting out a given number (K.CC), comparing groups and numerals, adding and subtracting within ten, decomposing numbers, making ten, teen numbers as ten and some more (K.NBT), comparing length and weight, sorting and counting by attribute (K.MD), and naming flat and solid shapes, position words, and composing shapes (K.G).

Designed for a child who cannot read yet

  • Every question is read aloud using the iPad's built-in voice. Nothing needs to be read to play.
  • No keyboard, ever. Answers are given by tapping a large card or dragging rocks into the cargo bay.
  • No timers, no lives, no losing. A wrong answer is a gentle nudge and a second try, with a spoken hint after two misses.

What it will not do

  • It does not go online. There is no networking code in the app at all.
  • It does not collect anything. No account, no name, no free text, no analytics.
  • It does not ask for more money. One purchase, then nothing else — no in-app purchases, no subscription, no content locked behind a second payment.
  • It does not grade your child. Scores and percentages live in the Parent Zone; the child's side shows only what they have collected and explored.
