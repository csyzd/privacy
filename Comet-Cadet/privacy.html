Privacy Policy

Comet Cadet does not collect any data. Not anonymised data, not aggregated data, not "technical" data. None.

The app contains no networking code. It cannot send anything anywhere, because there is nothing in it that is capable of making a network request. There is no account to create, no server to talk to, no analytics package, no advertising, and no third-party software of any kind inside it.

What the app stores, and where

Your child's progress — which surveys they have finished, which rocks and badges they have collected, and what difficulty each skill is set to — is written to a file inside the app's own folder on your iPad. Your settings for narration, sound, vibration, and the daily time limit are stored the same way. Both stay on the device. If you back your iPad up through iCloud or a computer, that backup includes this file, in the same way it includes every other app's files; that backup belongs to you, and we have no access to it.

The app never asks for your child's name or any other text. The only thing your child chooses about themselves is which of six astronaut pictures to use, and that choice is stored as a number.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for children. Because it collects nothing and transmits nothing, there is no personal information from a child to disclose, share, or sell, and no way for anyone to contact a child through it. There are no advertisements and no in-app purchases. It contains no links out to the web except the ones in the Parent Zone, which is protected by a gate that requires reading.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions at all. It does not use the camera, the microphone, location, contacts, photos, or notifications.

Deleting everything

Deleting the app removes the progress file with it. You can also erase everything from inside the app: Parent Zone, then Start Over, then Reset all progress.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here and shipped inside the app at the same time.
